Route Calculation Help for Accounting — 1.6.14

Invoice dates

The invoice form now provides separate selectors for the issue date, service date, and payment due date. The issue date defaults to the current date and is saved with the invoice, printed on the PDF, and used in Minimax XML.

Previously saved invoices without an explicit issue date continue to use their original creation date.

Additional costs

The Add to entered gross price mode is selected by default. In this mode, the entered gross price is the transport price and additional costs increase the final invoice total.

Select Included in entered gross price to retain the previous calculation, where additional costs are deducted from the entered gross price before the transport base and VAT are calculated.

Invoices saved before this option was introduced automatically retain the previous included-in-gross calculation when restored.
